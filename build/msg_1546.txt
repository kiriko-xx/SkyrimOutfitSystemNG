1.5.46: generate outfits from a plugin with a prefix tree, a character level LCP and slot constraints

Replaces the 1.5.45 rule (first four words as the costume name) which assumed
separators and could not tell two costumes apart when they only differ late in
the name.

- normalise for comparison only: ASCII lowercase, drop every separator, ignore
  tags in [] and (); the original spelling is kept for the outfit name.
- build a character level prefix tree and split a prefix only when every branch
  under it already forms a costume of its own (two pieces or more over two slots
  or more); otherwise the whole subtree stays one costume.
- bucket the pieces of a costume by slot, strip the shared part of each bucket
  and keep the differentiator (A / B / red / black); single piece slots are
  shared by every combination.
- combine only identical differentiators across slots; unmatched costumes write
  no preset at all and say so in the log, two pieces on one slot drops that
  combination, the same name on one slot keeps the first piece.
- names and limits unchanged: <plugin> <costume> / <plugin> _1, 96 outfits,
  32 variants, every outfit still needs its slots equipped (XXXO).

Stage 1 helpers are fenced with sentinel comments so tests/esp_outfits_trie.ps1
can compile them verbatim and assert the behaviour.
