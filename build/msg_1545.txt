1.5.45: pick golden pieces out of a plugin list, group generated outfits by costume name

- golden items: drop the hand typed local form id (ParseLocalId, ##goldenlocalid) and
  add RenderGoldenPieceList: pick a plugin, the page lists every slot armour of it,
  click a row to add that single piece. The editor of a group shows the same list for
  the plugin of that group. Rows use a row number id, the two lists use PushID(scope).
- outfits from a plugin: group the slot armour by the longest word prefix shared by
  at least two pieces (at most four words, bracketed tags ignored), split every
  costume by slot conflicts as before. A plugin with one costume keeps the old names,
  several costumes get "<plugin> <costume>". 96 outfits, 32 variants.
