1.5.36 test build: poll the morphs from any thread, bake from the engine worn pass

1.5.35 asked every poll who the thread was and threw the poll away when the answer
was "not the game thread". One session shows what that costs: 640 of 1241 polls were
rejected, in runs of up to 33 s, and the longest runs sat exactly on the seconds a
body morph slider was dragged - the illusion kept the shape the drag started from and
only refreshed once the player opened a menu (a menu pass is what finally gets a task
onto the game thread).

- OnGameThread() now only classifies and logs; the poll runs from the thread that
  asks for it (timer, or the skinning hooks).
- DrainPending() is serialised with a mutex: two threads can ask for a bake now.
- Only the repairs that drive the engine from outside the morph path (hair tint,
  provider dispatch, reillusion, the equip refresh behind a dropped illusion) stay
  main-thread only.
- New SkinTick(): the poll+bake is also driven from ShouldOverrideSkinning(), i.e.
  from inside the engine worn pass, which runs continuously while a slider is dragged
  and on the engine own thread. This is the anchor that replaces the dead per-frame
  pump.
- Removed the 1.5.35 FramePump (Main::Update id 35565/36564 has no call site in this
  executable) and the whole FindCallsInModule scan that only it used, and the SKSE
  AddTask path in Tick: no more task spam that gets thrown away.

Guard tests: morph_thread_gate rewritten for the new design, generic observer anchor
moved to the poll body. 7/7 pass.

Test package: SkyrimOutfitSystemNG-1.5.36-test.zip (single DLL, MO2 installable).
Not tagged, not merged into main: this stays on candidate/menu-morph-live until the
reporting machine confirms the drag follows.
