1.5.40: fix the empty ESP list and the golden items crash

- PluginScan: the clothing test looked at the male model only, so female-only
  clothing packs were all rejected and the outfit generator's plugin list was
  filtered down to nothing. The verdict now checks both sexes and accepts an
  editor id or a full name; the list filters on any slot armour instead of on
  the clothing count.
- I18n: translations are handed to printf as format strings, and the Chinese
  golden item lines had their placeholders swapped ('%zu piece(s) of %s added.'
  against '已添加 %s 的 %zu 件。'), so %s received a size_t and the game crashed
  in RenderGolden. Both lines now keep the English order and Apply() rejects any
  translation whose placeholder sequence differs, falling back to English.
